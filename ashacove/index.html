Good questions

A few things to know.

Does Ashacove connect to my bank?

No. Add records yourself or import a CSV. Ashacove does not access bank accounts, move money, or place trades.

Can I use it offline?

You can work with your locally saved records offline. New BTC, gold, and silver quotes need internet access. Downloading the app and App Store services also require a connection.

Can I use another currency?

Choose from 30 workspace currencies. Changing the currency relabels amounts; it does not convert existing numbers. Market-priced BTC, gold, and silver remain USD estimates.

Will I be able to move to another Mac?

Export a password-protected backup, then restore it on the other Mac. Keep the backup password safe: it cannot be reset by the developer.
