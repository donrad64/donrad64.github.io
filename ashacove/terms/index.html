App Store licensing

When Ashacove is offered through the Mac App Store, app licensing is governed by the license terms shown with the app. Unless a different license is provided there, Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement applies. Purchases and refund requests are handled through Apple. This page is product information; it does not replace the applicable store license or your statutory rights.

A planning tool, not financial advice

Ashacove helps you organize records you enter. It does not connect to bank accounts, transfer money, pay bills, place trades, or provide individualized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Check important decisions against your own statements and qualified advice where appropriate.

Totals depend on your entries, chosen month, and scope. Budget remainders are not live bank balances. Planned records are not confirmation that money was received or paid.

Mortgage and market estimates

Mortgage calculations are designed for US mortgages. Interest, escrow, insurance, fees, payment timing, and lender rules can affect actual results. Keep the principal balance, rate, monthly principal-and-interest payment, and next unpaid month current. Estimates do not replace your lender’s statements.

BTC, gold, and silver quotes come from a third party and may be delayed, stale, incorrect, or unavailable. Gold and silver use troy ounces. Market assets are estimated in USD; changing workspace currency does not perform conversion.

Your records and copies

Keep records accurate and create encrypted backups regularly. Protect your backup password and Mac’s encryption key. The developer cannot recover them. CSV exports and reports are readable files; protect copies you share or store outside the app.

Read the privacy policy for local storage, quote requests, support, and website hosting details.
