Privacy policy

CallPerch · Effective October 4, 2026

SupportPrivacy

CallPerch is created by Arash Manafirad (KR4GOJ). It searches public amateur-radio licensing records published by the United States Federal Communications Commission (FCC).

Data on your device

Your search queries, local FCC database, watchlist, and preferences remain on your device. CallPerch has no developer-operated accounts, analytics, advertising, or telemetry. Watchlist reminders are local operating-system notifications.

FCC data and connections

Sync connects directly to FCC download servers. As with other web requests, these connections expose network information such as your IP address to the service provider. Research uses the downloaded database and does not send your queries to the developer.

Public FCC records may contain names and mailing addresses. CallPerch does not independently collect or verify these addresses. Shared mailing addresses do not verify shared residence. Contact the FCC to correct licensing records.

External websites

FCC login and other external links open your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies. Direct-distribution versions include an optional Buy Me a Coffee link; the App Store candidate omits it. Payment information is handled by that website and is never received by CallPerch.

Removing local data

You can remove watchlist entries or disable reminders inside the app. Local preferences and databases can be removed using your operating system's application-data controls. Uninstalling may leave local application data behind.

This support website

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, when you visit this website. See GitHub's privacy statement. These pages do not include analytics or advertising scripts.

Contact

For privacy questions about CallPerch, contact kr4goj@arrl.net. Please email privacy-sensitive details instead of posting them in public GitHub issues.
