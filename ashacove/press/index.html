Ashacove is a personal finance app for Mac that brings monthly income, expenses, savings, assets, and debts into one calm workspace. It is designed for people who want to track their own records without connecting bank accounts.

Short descriptions

One line

A calm monthly workspace for your money, with financial records encrypted on your Mac.

Product description

Plan recurring income and bills, review upcoming due dates, compare months, and keep assets and debts in view. Ashacove supports reviewed CSV imports, password-protected backups, and optional market estimates for BTC, gold, and silver. No bank connection or Ashacove account is required.
