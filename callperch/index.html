Getting started

Search a callsign or licensee name. Browse Available estimates and Coming available, then select a callsign to inspect FCC records, applications, and other callsigns sharing its exact mailing address.

Use Sync FCC to refresh the weekly data. This downloads large archives and can take several minutes. Keep the app open until the import finishes. Imported FCC ZIP files are also supported.

Watchlist reminders

Star a callsign and allow notifications. Reminders use estimated dates from the current snapshot; sync regularly and verify availability and eligibility with the FCC before applying.

Common questions

No matching address? Full mailing-address fields must match. Unit numbers and PO boxes remain distinct. Missing or incomplete addresses cannot be grouped.

Why does a call look available? Dates are estimates based on cancellation/expiration records and may change with later assignments or FCC exceptions. These results are not FCC decisions.

Sync failed? Check your internet connection and available storage. Imports need several GB for the database and temporary archives. The previous snapshot is preserved if import fails.
