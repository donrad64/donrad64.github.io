Getting started

Search a callsign or licensee name. Browse Available estimates and Coming available, then select a callsign to inspect FCC records, applications, and other callsigns sharing its exact mailing address.

Use Sync FCC to refresh the weekly data. This downloads large archives and can take several minutes. At least 12 GiB of free space is required; each ZIP is limited to 1 GiB. A progress window shows the current stage and blocks other app controls while syncing or importing. Use Cancel Sync to stop safely and retain your previous snapshot. The result stays visible until you select Done. Keep the app open until the import finishes. Imported FCC ZIP files are also supported.

Callsign comparisons

Linux 1.3.0 includes the free comparison workspace: up to ten callsigns, adjustable priorities, personal ratings, local CW and spoken phonetics, QSL/plate previews, named saved comparisons, and CSV export. Open Compare callsigns in the sidebar or Add to comparison in details. Lower scores indicate your preferences; they do not establish FCC eligibility or availability.

Comparison drafts and saved ratings stay on your device. Linux speech uses local eSpeak and may sound synthetic. The voice-quality guide explains optional standalone Piper downloads. These do not automatically change CallPerch’s Speak phonetics button.

Snapshot-age reminders

A reminder appears when the local snapshot is at least seven days old, with stronger wording after thirty days. Dismissing it hides the reminder for that snapshot and local day. Sync regularly; snapshot data is not live FCC data.

Callsign assignment history

Repeated callsign switching summarizes at least two returns to previously held callsigns across four or more assignments linked by the same individual FRN. Expand Assignment timeline to inspect grant and cancellation dates; collapse it to hide the list. Smaller orange notes mark returns before the ordinary cancellation-based release estimate.

This is a limited historical summary, not a finding about intent, improper conduct, or compliance with FCC rules. It does not confirm former-holder eligibility, reserve a callsign, or predict an application outcome. Missing records, changed FRNs, and weekly snapshot timing may hide or affect patterns. No qualifying pattern does not establish that no switching occurred. Verify dates and eligibility directly with the FCC. Older snapshots need a fresh Sync FCC to add the identity fields; unavailable individual FRNs cannot be assessed.

Linux downloads

Download CallPerch Linux 1.3.0: choose amd64 for Intel/AMD or arm64 for a 64-bit ARM/Raspberry Pi system. The Ubuntu 24.04-based packages include checksums and portable archives. Debian packages include Python and Qt. An upgrade uses your existing local data and watchlist; close older app instances before upgrading or syncing.

Linux temporary storage during sync

Linux 1.3.0 stages downloaded FCC ZIPs in a temporary subfolder beside the database, then removes them after completion, cancellation or failure. It no longer depends on the size of a RAM-backed /tmp folder. The conservative 12 GiB requirement is available space for synchronization on the database filesystem, not the final database size. Errors identify the folder being checked.

For older Linux versions, the temporary-storage troubleshooting note explains the TMPDIR workaround. New versions reuse your existing database and settings without needing that override.

Linux development source

The Linux application and shared Python FCC engine are available under the MIT license in callperch-linux on GitHub. The repository includes build instructions, synthetic tests, contribution guidance, and private security-reporting contacts. Dependencies retain their own licenses. Downloaded FCC data is not included.

Watchlist reminders

Star a callsign to save it. On Mac, allow notifications for reminders. Linux notifications require a desktop notification service; reminders while the app is closed require the optional user timer described in the Linux instructions. Reminders use estimates from the current snapshot; verify availability and eligibility with the FCC before applying.

Common questions

No matching address? Full mailing-address fields must match. Unit numbers and PO boxes remain distinct. Missing or incomplete addresses cannot be grouped.

Why does a call look available? Dates are estimates based on cancellation/expiration records and may change with later assignments or FCC exceptions. These results are not FCC decisions.

Sync failed? Check your internet connection and available storage. Imports need several GB for the database and temporary archives. The previous snapshot is preserved if import fails.

Delete local data

Use Delete local data in the app to remove downloaded databases, comparisons, preferences, watchlist entries, and scheduled reminders. Exported CSV files remain where you saved them. Linux packages do not include an FCC database; versions that include a bundled public snapshot retain it until uninstalled. See the privacy policy for manual deletion paths.
