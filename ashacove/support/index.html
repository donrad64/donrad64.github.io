Let’s get you unstuck.

Check the guide and common questions below. If something still doesn’t work, open a GitHub issue with your app version, macOS version, and steps to reproduce using fictional examples.

GitHub Issues are public. Never post your vault, backup, password, bank details, encryption key, or screenshots with financial or personal information.

Open an Ashacove support issue ↗

A GitHub account is needed to post. Independent developer support is offered as available; no response-time guarantee is made.

Common questions

The app didn’t lock after sleep.

Open Ashacove → App Lock and choose Require Unlock. No App Lock disables automatic locking. Reopen the app and test again. You can also lock manually from the Ashacove menu.

I lost my backup password.

Backup passwords cannot be reset. If the original app still opens, export a new backup with a password you can keep safely. Do not delete working records or Keychain keys while troubleshooting.

My CSV import doesn’t look right.

Use UTF-8 and check the column mapping, number/date formats, workspace currency, and duplicate warnings in the preview. Import no more than 1,000 rows or 2 MB at once. No records are added until you confirm the import.

A market price is missing or old.

New quotes need internet access, and the provider can be unavailable. Check the timestamp and try Refresh prices. Values are estimates; a saved quote may be stale.

Is Money left my bank balance?

No. It is the remainder of the income and expenses entered for the selected scope and month. Ashacove is not connected to your bank.

Why did changing currency leave my numbers unchanged?

Workspace currency changes labels, not numerical values. No exchange-rate conversion occurs. Market-priced assets remain separate USD estimates when the workspace uses another currency.

Does deleting the app delete every copy of my data?

Not necessarily. Local app data, Keychain items, exported files, cloud folders, and system backups can remain. Keep a verified encrypted backup before removing anything you may need.

How do App Store purchases and refunds work?

Apple handles purchases. Once the app is available, consult your Apple purchase history and Apple’s purchase support for refund requests.

Privacy or security questions

Use the privacy contact for a general question or to request an appropriate private reporting channel. Do not publish exploit details or sensitive information in a public support issue.
