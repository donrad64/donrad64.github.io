Getting started

A few familiar numbers are enough to begin. Here’s a practical path through your first month.

The first App Store release is in preparation. This guide describes the current app; final release details will appear here when it is available.

01

Choose your first month

Open Ashacove and follow the setup guide. Choose the month and workspace currency you want to use. Start with a manageable set of familiar numbers.

Use one workspace currency consistently. Changing it later does not convert existing amounts.

02

Add income and regular bills

Add your usual income and expenses, then use the monthly worksheet to adjust amounts. Recurring items give future months a starting point; one-off items belong only where you add them.

For example: Salary $4,500; Rent $1,600; Groceries $450. These are fictional examples, not recommended budgets.

03

Give bills a due date

Use Money out to review bills. Mark a payment only when you have made it. Choose days until due to bring approaching due dates into view.

Money left is your entered budget remainder. It is not a live bank balance, and Ashacove does not make payments.

04

Build your bigger picture

Add savings and other assets, then debts such as a mortgage or loan. Enter balances from your own records and keep them current. Eligible linked payments can update tracked balances.

Market pricing is optional. Gold and silver pricing uses troy ounces; live market estimates are USD-based. US mortgage estimates exclude assumptions your lender may handle differently.

05

Enable App Lock

In the Ashacove menu, open App Lock and choose Require Unlock. Ashacove uses Touch ID or your Mac password. With protection enabled, it locks after sleep and after five minutes without activity.

If you choose No App Lock, automatic locking is disabled. After enabling protection, test sleep and wake and confirm the app asks you to unlock.

06

Make your first backup

Open Backup & restore. Choose a backup password of at least 12 characters and save the encrypted backup somewhere safe. Keep its password separately and make fresh backups regularly.

The developer cannot recover a missing encryption key or reset a lost backup password. CSV and HTML report exports are readable files, not encrypted backups.

07

Bring in existing records

Choose Import CSV. Select a UTF-8 CSV, map its columns, and check the preview. Review possible duplicates and errors before confirming the import.

Imports support up to 1,000 rows and 2 MB at a time. Use the same currency as the workspace; the import does not perform currency conversion.

Keep it simple

Return once a week to update payments, review upcoming bills, and check your balances. Your yearly history becomes more useful as you keep adding records.
