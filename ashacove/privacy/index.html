Privacy policy

What stays on your Mac, what leaves it, and the choices you control.

Effective date: October 16, 2026. This policy covers the Ashacove Mac app and this website. Ashacove is developed by Arash Manafirad, who maintains the GitHub account donrad64.

Your local financial records

Ashacove stores income, expenses, assets, debts, and settings on your Mac. Saved financial records are encrypted locally. The developer does not receive your financial records, bank credentials, backup passwords, or encryption keys through the app. No Ashacove account or bank connection is required.

Imported files are processed on your Mac. The app does not include an advertising SDK or developer-operated app analytics. The app uses a private service on your Mac to display and manage records; it is not an online account or a developer-hosted financial database.

Encryption and App Lock

Saved records and newly exported backups are encrypted. The local encryption key is stored using macOS Keychain. Optional App Lock uses macOS authentication and blocks access through the app’s local service while locked. Enable Require Unlock in the App Lock menu to use it.

Locking preserves drafts and decrypted working data in process memory so you can resume. It does not erase memory or protect a compromised Mac. No security measure can guarantee protection in every circumstance.

Optional market-price requests

If you use market pricing for BTC, gold, or silver, Ashacove requests public quotes directly from Gold API over HTTPS. The request includes the requested ticker (BTC, XAU, or XAG). It does not include your quantities, balances, account names, or financial history.

Active market assets can cause automatic price refreshes while you use the app; requests are not limited to pressing Refresh. You can use manual assets without market pricing if you do not want these quote requests.

Gold API’s published policy describes collecting API requests, timestamps, and IP addresses, and possibly approximate location inferred from IP addresses, for purposes including service operation, security, and usage analysis. The provider controls its own processing and retention. Read the Gold API privacy policy.

Backups, exports, and retention

New backups are exported with a password you choose. Keep that password safe; the developer cannot reset it. CSV exports and HTML reports are readable files and can reveal financial information to anyone who can access them. Protect them and review them before sharing.

Records and local recovery copies remain on your Mac until you remove them. Exported files remain wherever you saved them, which may include a cloud folder or system backup you control. Removing the app may not remove its saved data, Keychain item, or exported copies. Make a verified backup before deleting local app data or keys. Historical backups and snapshots may retain earlier copies.

Apple services

Apple handles Mac App Store purchases and distribution under its own terms and privacy policy. The developer does not receive your payment-card details through Ashacove.

This website and GitHub

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, external font requests, newsletter forms, or site-owned cookies or local storage. GitHub can process technical information such as IP addresses and request details when serving the site, under its privacy statement. Following external links brings you to services with their own privacy practices.

Support and privacy questions

Support is handled through GitHub Issues. If you choose to post an issue, the developer receives the GitHub username and information you include. Issues and comments are public and may remain available after the issue is closed, including in copies outside the developer’s control.

Ask general questions only. Do not post financial records, passwords, encryption keys, personal identifiers, or unredacted screenshots. For a matter requiring confidential details, first ask how to arrange an appropriate private channel without including those details in the public issue.

Support information is used to understand and respond to requests and maintain the app. No independent copy of your financial database is collected by the app. GitHub controls platform retention; you can edit or remove your own content subject to GitHub’s controls, and ask the maintainer about content they control. Depending on your location, you may have privacy rights; use the contact below to ask about information the developer holds. Only you control local financial records on your Mac.
